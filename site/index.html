Why Kubri

Everything k9s does

Every resource and CRD, live. Drill-downs, logs, shells, port-forwards, Pulses, XRay, RBAC, Helm, the sanitizer and benchmarks. Your k9s aliases, hotkeys, plugins and skins keep working.

Keyboard or mouse

: opens the command palette and ? lists every key. Or just click: each action is a button, and a right click on a row shows them all.

Desktop or browser

A small native app for Windows, macOS and Linux. Or a container for your team with a sign-in, or a pod in the cluster with its own service account.

A closer look

Install

Desktop app

Windows (.msi, -setup.exe), macOS (.dmg) and Linux (.deb, .rpm, .AppImage) from the latest release. Kubri uses the kubeconfig on your computer, just like kubectl, and updates itself.

In the browser, with Docker

docker run --rm -p 127.0.0.1:7878:7878 \
  --user "$(id -u)" \
  --mount "type=bind,src=$HOME/.kube/config,dst=/kubeconfig,readonly" -e KUBECONFIG=/kubeconfig \
  -e KUBRI_PASSWORD='a long password' \
  ghcr.io/snaketech-tu/kubri

Then open http://127.0.0.1:7878 and sign in with the password.

In a cluster

kubectl apply -f https://raw.githubusercontent.com/snaketech-tu/kubri/main/deploy/kubernetes.yaml
kubectl -n kubri create secret generic kubri-password --from-literal=password='a long password'
kubectl -n kubri port-forward svc/kubri 7878:80

Then open http://127.0.0.1:7878. Kubri sees what its service account may see.
