# Code of conduct

Kubri is made by people who want working with Kubernetes to be pleasant. Please keep working on Kubri pleasant too.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to Kubernetes, Rust or open source.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you judge.
- Criticise ideas and code, not people.
- Accept a "no" to a change gracefully, and give reasons when you say one.

## What is not welcome

- Insults, harassment, threats and personal attacks.
- Unwelcome sexual attention, and jokes or remarks about anyone's gender, sexuality, origin, religion, disability, age or appearance.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Repeatedly pushing a point after you have been asked to stop.

## Where it applies

In issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere else you speak for the project.

## Reporting

Tell the maintainer, snaketech-tu on GitHub, privately, for example through a private [security report](https://github.com/snaketech-tu/kubri/security) if nothing else fits. Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may edit or remove comments, and may block people from the project, as fits what happened.

This code is based on the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.
